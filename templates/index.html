How Nhealth Works

Your Healthcare. Connected in One Place.
From your first consultation to diagnostics, medicines, emergency support, dental care, hospital assistance and ongoing home healthcare — Nhealth brings your healthcare journey together through one connected platform.

01

Tell Us What You Need

Start by choosing the healthcare service you need or simply tell us about your requirement.

Online Doctor Consultation Doctor Home Visit Lab Tests at Home X-Ray & Diagnostics Pharmacy Delivery Dental Care Ambulance Services Blood Support Hospital OPD Assistance Premium OPD Physiotherapy Eldercare Bedside Assistance Home Healthcare Pick-up & Drop Procedure Assistance

Whatever your healthcare requirement, start with Nhealth.

02

Get Connected to the Right Care

Based on the service you select and the requirement you provide, Nhealth helps connect you with an appropriate healthcare professional, provider or service.

  • Doctor Consultations: Explore available doctors and select a suitable specialist.
  • Other Services: Nhealth coordinates directly with verified healthcare providers and service partners.

One platform. Multiple healthcare services. Connected care.

03

Share Your Details

Provide the information required for your selected service. This helps Nhealth and the relevant healthcare provider understand your requirement and coordinate the next step.

Patient Details Symptoms / Concerns Preferred Service Location Appointment Time Prescription / Info

Tell us what you need. We'll help coordinate the care.

04

Book Your Service

Once your service and provider are confirmed, review the booking details and complete the required payment.

Receive instant verified booking confirmation of your:

Consultation Home Visit Diagnostic Test Medicine Order Dental Appointment Ambulance Service Hospital/OPD Home Care

Simple booking. Clear information. Convenient access.

05

Receive Your Healthcare Service

Your selected healthcare service is then delivered or coordinated seamlessly according to your booking.

🩺

Doctor Consultation

Connect with a doctor through online consultation or arrange a doctor home visit.

🧪

Diagnostics at Home

Arrange laboratory sample collection and supported diagnostic services at home.

💊

Pharmacy

Get prescribed medicines and healthcare essentials coordinated for doorstep delivery.

🦷

Dental Care

Book and coordinate dental consultations and supported dental services.

🚑

Ambulance Services

Request and coordinate ambulance transportation when required.

🩸

Blood Support

Access blood-related support and coordination through the verified Nhealth network.

🏥

Hospital & OPD Assistance

Coordinate hospital OPDs, appointments, healthcare procedures, reports and medicines.

👵

Home Healthcare

Arrange supported services such as physiotherapy, eldercare and bedside assistance.

Healthcare support that reaches beyond the consultation.

06

Manage Your Reports & Healthcare Information

Following consultations, diagnostics and other healthcare services, relevant prescriptions, reports and documents are made available through your Nhealth healthcare journey.

This keeps important medical records organized and easily accessible for future consultations and follow-up care.

Your healthcare journey, organized in one place.

07

Continue Your Care

Healthcare is an ongoing journey. After your initial service, Nhealth can help coordinate supported follow-up requirements.

Follow-up Consultations Lab Tests Diagnostic Services Medicines Physiotherapy Dental Care Home Healthcare Hospital Assistance Eldercare Bedside Assistance

From your first requirement to continuing care, Nhealth stays connected to your healthcare journey.

About Nhealth

Powering the Future of Healthcare at Home

Accessible, Connected, Convenient & Closer to Home

At Nhealth, we believe healthcare should be accessible, connected, convenient, and closer to home.

Nhealth is an integrated Healthcare-at-Home platform designed to connect patients with the healthcare services they need through one convenient digital ecosystem. From consulting a doctor online to arranging healthcare services at home, we aim to simplify the entire healthcare journey for patients and their families.

Whether you need a medical consultation, diagnostic test, medicines, physiotherapy, dental care, eldercare, an ambulance, or assistance with hospital procedures, Nhealth is designed to help you access the right healthcare service through a single platform.

Our Vision

Connected Care Anytime, Anywhere

To build a connected healthcare ecosystem where people can access essential healthcare services anytime, anywhere, and as close to home as possible.

We envision a future where technology makes healthcare coordination simpler—not more complicated—and where patients and families can find the support they need through one trusted platform.

Our Mission

Accessible, Convenient & Unified Care

Our mission is to make healthcare more accessible and convenient by connecting patients, doctors, diagnostic centres, pharmacies, hospitals, caregivers, and other healthcare service providers through a unified digital platform.

  • Make healthcare services easier to discover and access.
  • Reduce the effort involved in coordinating multiple healthcare services.
  • Bring appropriate healthcare services closer to patients' homes.
  • Support elderly people, families, and patients who need assistance.
  • Connect healthcare providers with patients through technology.
  • Create a seamless healthcare journey from consultation to diagnosis, treatment, and follow-up.

Built Around People

Putting Patients & Families First

At Nhealth, technology is only a tool. The real purpose is to make healthcare more human, accessible, and convenient.

We are building Nhealth with patients and their families at the centre of the experience, while creating opportunities for doctors, laboratories, pharmacies, hospitals, caregivers, ambulance providers, and other healthcare professionals to become part of a connected healthcare network.

Our Commitment

Convenience, Transparency & Accessibility

Nhealth is being built with a focus on convenience, transparency, accessibility, and responsible healthcare coordination.

We aim to continuously improve the platform, expand our healthcare network, and develop services that can genuinely make healthcare easier for individuals and families.
