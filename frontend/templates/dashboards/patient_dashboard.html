Frequently Asked Questions

How do I avail free consultations once I subscribe?

Once you activate your OPD Membership, your account is automatically upgraded to VIP status. Whenever you click "Consult Doctor" or select a specialist in the dashboard, the consultation fee will be automatically zeroed out (₹0).

Can I include my elderly parents who live in another town?

Yes! Both Gold Family and Platinum VIP plans allow you to add family members across Andhra Pradesh. They will receive their own Digital OPD Pass ID to book consultations, lab tests, and Health Support Riders in their local area.

How does the Health Support Rider benefit work?

Gold Plan includes 2 free escort trips each month, and Platinum includes unlimited free trips. Whenever you book a Health Support Rider to take a patient to the hospital or clinic, your monthly free balance will be automatically credited.
